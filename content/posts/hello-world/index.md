## introduction

Welcome to my first blog post! Since this platform serves as both my portfolio and my personal website, I want to introduce the two distinct sides of me: my professional life and my personal world.

## professional

I recently graduated from Ho Technical University with a BTech in Computer Science. More than anything else, I love solving problems. I am obsessed with that specific dopamine hit you get when a broken piece of code finally works, and I chase that feeling constantly.

However, I am also a deeply creative person. I've realized that I don't enjoy purely technical, dry programming. I need a creative payoff.

That is exactly why I love web development. It perfectly balances technical logic with visual design, especially in front-end work. I love spending time in Figma, designing custom icons, and making user interfaces look and feel just right. In fact, I am seriously considering going all-in on UI/UX design.

## personal

Following that same mix of creativity and visual problem-solving, I love playing video games—and I love making them. For now, game development is a hobby, but I definitely wouldn't mind going pro someday.

I also watch movies. A lot of movies. A LOT OF MOVIES.

As you might have figured out by now, I am a highly visual person. If you love striking visuals, storytelling, and design, it doesn't get much better than cinema. movies.

## conclusion

This website is going to be a space where these two worlds collide: technical problem-solving and my creative hobbies. I love yapping about the things I enjoy, so expect a lot of deep dives into tech, UI design, games, and film.

Thanks for reading, and stay tuned!
