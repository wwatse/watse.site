introduction

welcome to my first blog post. since this platform serves as both my portfolio and my personal website, i want to introduce the two distinct sides of me: my professional life and my personal world.

professional

i recently graduated from ho technical university with a btech in computer science. more than anything else, i love solving problems. i'm obsessed with that dopamine hit when a broken piece of code finally works, and i chase it constantly.

i'm also a deeply creative person. purely technical, dry programming doesn't interest me. i need a creative payoff.

that's what draws me to systems engineering, especially where it meets graphics and human interfaces. i like building the layer between a system and the person using it: rendering pipelines, developer tools, editor plugins. right now i'm learning godot plugins and linux applets, and the open-source tools i use daily are the ones i'd most like to give back to.

personal

same mix of creativity and technical problem-solving: i love playing video games and making them. game dev is a hobby for now, but i wouldn't mind going pro someday.

i also watch movies. a lot of them.

i'm a highly visual person. if you love striking visuals, storytelling, and design, it doesn't get much better than cinema.

conclusion

this website is going to be a space where these two worlds collide: technical problem-solving and my creative hobbies. i love yapping about the things i enjoy, so expect a lot of deep dives into systems, graphics, games, and film.

thanks for reading, and stay tuned.
